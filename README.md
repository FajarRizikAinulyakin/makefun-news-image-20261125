This repository holds a single image: a 1024 by 1024 newspaper front
page for Wednesday 25 November 2026, published under the MIT license.

The front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", sums up the day's world news:

- **Lead:** Houthi fighters hit Riyadh's tallest tower, the 385-metre
  PIF headquarters, for the second time in five days — 11 people
  wounded (Reuters, AFP).
- **Cyberattack:** the CIA and the Office of the Director of National
  Intelligence were hit by a cyber breach (CNN).
- **Tech trial:** a New York jury found Huawei guilty of stealing trade
  secrets on 10 of 12 counts (US Department of Justice, AFP).
- **Gaza:** an Israeli strike killed a Hamas official; Gaza's death
  toll reached 74,746 (WAFA, Oxfam).
- **Ukraine war:** Russia called Ukraine's drone strikes on Russian
  ships "terrorism" after Kyiv confirmed them (Reuters).
- **Ebola:** the WHO evacuated health workers in DR Congo amid
  fighting, as the country's worst Ebola outbreak claimed 5,775 lives.

In brief: Sarkozy and Carla Bruni ordered to trial in France; NATO to
send early-warning aircraft to Poland; Trump awarded Rudy Giuliani the
Presidential Medal of Freedom.

## The program

`main.py` is a standard-library-only Python program. Run it and it
writes an exact, digest-checked copy of `entry.png` to the output
directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output` by default). It takes
no arguments, uses no network, and produces identical bytes on every
run.
